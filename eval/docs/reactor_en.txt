The Helion-3 research reactor produces 48 megawatts of thermal power and has operated since 2016. Its core contains 214 fuel assemblies arranged in a hexagonal grid. Coolant enters the core at 290 degrees Celsius and leaves at 325 degrees. The reactor is shut down for refuelling every 18 months, a process that takes 21 days. Operators follow Procedure HR-77 for emergency shutdowns, which must be completed within 90 seconds. The facility has recorded no safety incidents since commissioning. Staff receive refresher training twice a year.
