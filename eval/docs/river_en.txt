The River Tessal flows for 412 kilometres from the Grey Hills to the Bay of Orsa. Its basin covers 38,000 square kilometres and is home to about 2.1 million people. The river is crossed by 17 bridges, the oldest of which, the Arch of Danwick, dates from 1488. Each spring the Tessal floods the lower valley, and since 1976 a dam at Port Elmer has controlled the water level. The most common fish in the river is the silver chub, and fishing requires a licence. Boat trips run from May to September.
